Armaan Parvez

  • Founder & CEO
  • Product Designer
  • 4 yrs · 45+ products · 24+ countries
armaanux.in LinkedIn
“There are so many ways to book a trip in India, and almost none to help you decide where to go.”

Armaan loved travel long before he could actually do much of it. While researching trips across India, he kept running into the same problem. There were endless ways to book a hotel or a flight, but almost nothing to help him decide where he should go in the first place.

It bothered him that a country this big keeps getting reduced to the same few spots. India has some of the most varied landscapes, cultures and food anywhere, yet most people keep going back to the same crowded places, only because they never find out what else is out there.

So he started Raasta, to turn scattered, confusing research into one clear answer, and to give the country's overlooked places a way to be found.

A note from the founder

Why we’re building Raasta

Travel should feel exciting, not exhausting.

Think about the last time you wanted to take a break and visit somewhere new. What did you do first? Most of us open Google, scroll through Instagram reels, watch YouTube videos, read blogs, compare websites, and ask friends for recommendations. Hours later, we’re often left with more questions than answers.

  • How does the place actually look today?
  • Is it worth visiting right now?
  • Is it safe?
  • What should I expect when I get there?

That uncertainty is what inspired Raasta.

We’re building Raasta to become India’s trusted travel companion. A place where you can discover destinations, understand real conditions, know what to expect, and confidently decide where and when to travel. No endless tabs. No scattered information. Just everything you need in one place.

Our vision goes far beyond helping people plan trips. We want to map the beauty of India in a way that has never been done before. From famous destinations to hidden gems, we want every traveller to have access to reliable, up-to-date information that helps them travel smarter and safer.

As Raasta grows, we’ll continue introducing intelligent features powered by AI, personalized recommendations, safety tools, and a comprehensive destination library designed around real traveller needs.

At the heart of it all, our goal is simple: to build trust.

  • Trust that the information is accurate.
  • Trust that you’re making the right decision.
  • Trust that wherever your journey takes you, Raasta will help you get there with confidence.

This is just the beginning of a much larger vision, and we’re excited to build it for every traveller across India.

On behalf of the Raasta team
