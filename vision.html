Mapping India differently.

Booking a trip in India is a solved problem. Deciding where to go is not. Raasta is being built to own that decision: the first thing people open before a trip, to choose where to go, when, and whether it is even worth it.

01 The thesis

A billion people, thousands of places, and no good way to decide where to go. Discovery is the largest unsolved problem in Indian travel, and the one we are building Raasta to solve.

02 The problem

Discovery is broken
before the trip even starts.

Choosing where to go in India means stitching together a dozen tools that were never meant to work together. Every one of them shows you something. None of them gives you an answer.

Where people look today
  • Instagram reels
  • Travel blogs
  • Google Maps
  • Weather apps
  • Reddit threads
  • YouTube vlogs
  • AI chatbots
  • WhatsApp groups
Not one of them answers the only question that matters: where should I actually go?

So the same ten places stay crowded, hidden gems stay hidden, and a single trip takes evenings to plan.

03 Why now

The pieces did not exist
five years ago. They do now.

01

India is travelling like never before

Domestic travel has bounced back hard, and a new generation is looking well beyond the same few cities.

02

Discovery moved to feeds, not search

People find places on reels and posts, then lose hours turning that spark into a real, costed plan.

03

Curation is finally possible at scale

Better data and AI now let us score and explain destinations the way a well-travelled friend would.

04 The opportunity

A country this vast
deserves better discovery.

India is one of the most varied countries on earth, yet most trips funnel into a tiny set of destinations. The gap between what exists and what gets discovered is the entire opportunity.

A handful of places
soak up most of the trips
The rest of India
still waiting to be discovered

India has 28 states and 8 union territories. Raasta is being built for all 36, with 500+ destinations researched and scored for fit, and growing every week. Not just the famous few.
