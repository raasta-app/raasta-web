Three steps. One journey.

    Discover

    Choose

  3. 03

    Go

    Hit Start, and the route, timings and local picks ride along with you, stop by stop, to the last one.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Raasta?

Raasta is an upcoming travel app for India that turns scattered trip research into clear answers: curated trips and multi-stop journeys, each with real budgets, the best time to go, routes and what to watch for. It's currently in pre-launch.

When will Raasta launch?

We're building toward launch on iOS and Android. Join the waitlist and we'll email you the moment it's ready. We can't promise a fixed date yet.

How much will Raasta cost?

Pricing isn't finalised yet. Joining the waitlist is free, and waitlisters will be the first to hear about launch and any early-access offers.

What do I get by joining the waitlist?

Launch and early-access updates by email. We only use your details for Raasta updates (no spam), and you can opt out at any time.

Does Raasta book hotels or transport for me?

Not today. Raasta is a trip-intelligence app that helps you decide where, when and how to travel. It doesn't currently offer bookings, payments or accounts.

How do you handle my personal data?

We collect only your name, email and how you heard about us, stored securely to manage the waitlist. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
